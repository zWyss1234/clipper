Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who uses Clipper

Clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner. It has no public users, no sign-up and no website accounts.

Data Clipper handles

  • TikTok access token of the owner's own TikTok account, obtained through TikTok Login Kit with the scopes user.info.basic and video.upload. It is stored only on the owner's computer and used only to upload drafts to that account.
  • Public live-stream video and public chat messages of creators who allow clipping. Chat messages are counted in memory to detect highlight moments; they are not stored or shared. Recordings are kept on the owner's computer for at most 10 minutes, except the clips that are kept for publishing.

What Clipper does not do

  • It does not collect data about TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use cookies or analytics on this website.

Deletion

The owner can revoke Clipper's access at any time in TikTok (Settings › Security › Manage app permissions), which invalidates the stored token. Creators can ask for the removal of their clips.
