What Clipper is

Clipper is a personal, non-public tool used by its owner to prepare short vertical clips from live streams and send them as drafts to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

Content and permissions

  • Clipper is only used on streams whose creators allow clipping, under the conditions they set (for example, crediting their channel in the TikTok profile and in each caption).
  • Every clip credits the original creator and links to their channel.
  • The owner reviews each draft and publishes it manually on TikTok. Nothing is published automatically.
  • A creator who no longer wishes to be clipped can ask for removal; their clips will be deleted.
